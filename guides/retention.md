# How to keep retention (long-form YouTube)

CTR earns the click; retention decides whether the platform widens reach. This is a playbook for holding an audience
through a talking-head video. It is mostly a scripting concern, so share it with whoever writes the scripts.

## Two things to internalise first

1. **Retention is a shape, not a number.** Find the specific seconds where the line drops and fix those. Every video has
   2-3 killer moments: the first 30 s and one or two mid-video sags. Spreading effort evenly is the wrong instinct.
2. **You keep people by never letting the question feel answered**, not by entertaining harder. Stimulation (jump cuts,
   b-roll, SFX) is the weakest lever. The real glue is an open loop the viewer needs closed.

## Levers, strongest to weakest

1. **A story spine, not just an argument.** A story retains because the viewer waits for the outcome; an explainer
   retains only on "is this interesting". Give an argument a narrative shape: "I believed X. I went looking. Here is
   what I found." Receipts beat arguments.
2. **Never let the viewer feel the question is answered.** Keep one loop open at all times: close one, open the next.
   Resolve the title's promise LATE (in the last ~20%); naming the answer at minute 4 closes the gap and people leave.
   Do not release the tension early.
3. **Win the first 30-60 seconds.** Land the curiosity gap by ~second 8, confirm the topic fast, preview the map and
   withhold the mechanism.
4. **Re-hook at section seams.** Each section closes one loop and opens the next, so there is never a natural exit.
5. **Pacing, cuts and graphics.** Tight silences and a minimal graphic on the key beat help at the margin. Do them last.

## Diagnose from the curve

- Use the in-video audience-retention curve (YouTube Studio: Engagement), not average view duration over time.
- Look for: the first-30 s leak (hook), mid-video cliffs (a seam, or the promise resolving early), and spikes (a
  re-hook that worked: do more of that).

## When to judge

- Not in the first 24-48 h: early data is mostly subscribers, a biased sample.
- Read the retention shape at ~48 h for craft lessons. Judge performance (CTR, reach) after 48-72 h+ of cold-audience
  data. Swapping title or thumbnail too early resets the platform's learning.
- Benchmark against your median video, never your best outlier.
