# Hook frame counting: the 8-3-3-4-5 intro

A fast way to pull a viewer into a video's first second without a sit-down or a lens zoom: a sequence of very short
clips whose lengths, in frames, follow **8, 3, 3, 4, 5, then one long clip**.

- Each number is one clip, exactly that many frames long (at 25 fps the whole flurry is ~0.9 s).
- Use clean, strong shots with the subject centred in each, so the eye stays in one place while the picture changes.
- The final long clip is where the video actually starts (often the first spoken line).
- Cut by frame counts (`-frames:v`), never float durations, or the rhythm drifts.
- It pairs well with a click or whoosh on each cut, kept quiet under any music.

Use it for vlog and long-form openers; it is too busy for a podcast or a calm talking-head short.
